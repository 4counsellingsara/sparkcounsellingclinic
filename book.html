BEFORE YOUR APPOINTMENT

A few things to know.

Phone & Video Appointments

Counselling is currently available by phone or secure video. For video sessions, choose a private, comfortable space where you can speak freely.

Fees & Payment

Individual Counselling: $100 / 50 minutes.

Child & Youth Counselling: $100 / 50 minutes.

Couples Counselling: $130 / 50 minutes.

Payment is collected following your appointment.

E-transfer: info@sparkcounsellingclinic.com

Insurance Coverage

Professional registrations are currently pending.

Counselling services may not qualify for reimbursement under extended health insurance plans at this time.

Please confirm coverage directly with your insurer.

Cancellation Policy

Please provide at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule.

Late cancellations and missed appointments may be charged the full session fee, as outlined in your informed consent.

New Clients

Before your first counselling session, you'll receive intake and informed consent forms to review and complete.

These forms provide important information about the counselling process, confidentiality, fees, and clinic policies.

For Parents & Caregivers

Remote counselling may look different depending on your child's age, needs, and comfort with technology.

Caregiver support may be helpful for younger children. We can discuss suitability during a consultation.
