A few things to know.

Insurance Coverage

Cancellation Policy

Please provide at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule.

Late cancellations and missed appointments may be charged the full session fee, as outlined in your informed consent.

New Clients

Before your first counselling session, you'll receive intake and informed consent forms to review and complete.

These help me understand your needs and ensure you have important information about the counselling process.

For Parents & Caregivers

Virtual counselling may look different depending on your child's age, developmental needs, and comfort with technology.

Caregiver participation or support may be helpful for younger children. We can discuss your child's needs during an initial consultation.
