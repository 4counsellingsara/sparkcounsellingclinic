Complimentary Consultation

Free / 15 minutes

Taking the first step toward counselling can feel overwhelming, and it's important to find someone you feel comfortable with.

This complimentary 15-minute phone consultation is an opportunity for us to briefly connect, discuss what you're looking for, answer any questions you may have, and explore whether working together feels like a good fit.

SUPPORT FOR AGES 5 & UP

Child & Youth Counselling

$100 / 50 minutes

Growing up comes with big feelings that aren't always easy to understand or put into words. Counselling provides a supportive space for children and youth to explore their emotions, feel heard, and develop healthy ways to cope.

I offer support for anxiety, emotional regulation, family changes and separation, parenting-related challenges, and difficult or traumatic experiences.

Together, we can work toward building emotional awareness, coping skills, confidence, and resilience.

Sessions are tailored to each young person's developmental needs, with caregiver involvement discussed when appropriate.
