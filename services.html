BEFORE YOUR APPOINTMENT

A few things to know.

Here's some helpful information to make getting started a little easier.

Phone & Video Appointments

Counselling is currently available by phone or secure video.

For video sessions, I encourage you to find a private, comfortable space where you can speak freely.

Fees & Payment

Individual Counselling: $100 / 50 minutes.

Child & Youth Counselling: $100 / 50 minutes.

Couples Counselling: $130 / 50 minutes.

Payment is collected following your appointment.

E-transfers can be sent to info@sparkcounsellingclinic.com .

Insurance Coverage

My professional registrations are currently pending.

Counselling services may not currently qualify for reimbursement under extended health insurance plans.

Please confirm coverage directly with your insurance provider.

Cancellation Policy

Please provide at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment.

Late cancellations and missed appointments may be charged the full session fee, as outlined in your informed consent.

New Clients

Before your first counselling session, you'll receive intake and informed consent forms to review and complete.

These forms help me better understand your needs and provide important information about counselling, confidentiality, fees, and policies.

For Parents & Caregivers

Counselling for children and youth is tailored to their developmental needs and individual circumstances.

Caregiver involvement and suitability for remote counselling are considered individually. We can discuss these considerations during a consultation.
