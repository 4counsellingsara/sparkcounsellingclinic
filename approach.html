WORKING TOGETHER

My Approach to Counselling

I'm Sara Park, the counsellor behind Spark Counselling Clinic in Kelowna, BC. My approach is grounded in compassion, collaboration, and the belief that meaningful change happens when you feel genuinely seen, heard, and understood.

Counselling offers a space where your experiences are honoured, your voice matters, and you can explore meaningful change at your own pace.

MY PHILOSOPHY

You are the expert on your own experience.

I believe counselling works best when we approach it as a partnership.

You bring your experiences, values, and understanding of yourself. I bring curiosity, compassion, clinical knowledge, and a commitment to creating a supportive space.

Rather than assuming I know what's best for you, I want to understand what matters to you and explore possible paths forward together.

My goal is to create a counselling relationship built on trust, openness, collaboration, and respect for your autonomy.

Whether you're seeking support with anxiety, depression, relationships, life transitions, or personal growth, our work together is guided by your unique needs and goals.

FOUNDATIONS OF MY PRACTICE

What guides our work

My counselling approach is grounded in several guiding principles that shape how we work together.

01

Person-Centred

Offering empathy, genuine connection, and respect for your autonomy. Your experiences, perspectives, and goals remain central to our work together.

02

Trauma-Informed

Prioritizing emotional safety, choice, collaboration, and respect for boundaries. We move at a pace that feels appropriate for you.

03

Collaborative

Working together to explore experiences, identify goals, and find approaches that fit. Your feedback and preferences are an important part of the counselling process.

04

Culturally Humble

Respecting your identities, culture, relationships, values, and lived experiences. I strive to remain curious, reflective, and open to perspectives different from my own.

05

Strengths-Based

Making room for challenges while recognizing your resilience, resources, and capacity for growth. Counselling can help you reconnect with strengths you may have lost sight of.

06

Flexible & Integrative

Tailoring counselling to your needs rather than following a rigid formula. Different approaches may be incorporated depending on your experiences, goals, and preferences.

THERAPEUTIC APPROACHES

Drawing from different perspectives.

My counselling approach is integrative, meaning I draw from different therapeutic perspectives to support each person's individual needs and goals.

Depending on your experiences and preferences, I may incorporate ideas and strategies from Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT), Solution-Focused Brief Therapy (SFBT), narrative therapy, psychodynamic approaches, and Motivational Interviewing (MI).

These approaches offer different ways to understand emotions, relationships, behavioural patterns, personal experiences, and opportunities for change.

Some sessions may focus on developing practical coping strategies, while others may involve exploring emotions, past experiences, relationships, or patterns that influence your present life.

There is no single approach that works for everyone. We'll regularly check in about what feels helpful, and your feedback is always welcome.

BEGINNING COUNSELLING

What can you expect?

Starting counselling can feel unfamiliar, especially if it's your first time reaching out for support.

Our first sessions are an opportunity to get to know each other, understand what brought you to counselling, and begin exploring what you'd like to work toward.

You don't need to prepare the perfect explanation or know exactly what your goals are. We can explore those questions together.

I welcome questions and feedback about what is or isn't working for you. Counselling is a collaborative process, and your voice matters throughout.

If you're considering counselling in Kelowna, you're welcome to begin with a complimentary 15-minute phone consultation to ask questions and explore whether working together feels like a good fit.
