WORKING TOGETHER

My Approach to Counselling

A compassionate, collaborative space where your experiences are honoured, your voice matters, and meaningful change happens at your own pace.

MY PHILOSOPHY

You are the expert on your own experience.

I believe that counselling works best when we approach it as a partnership.

You bring your experiences, values, and understanding of yourself. I bring curiosity, compassion, clinical knowledge, and a commitment to creating a space where you feel supported.

Rather than assuming I know what's best for you, I want to understand what matters to you and explore possible paths forward together.

FOUNDATIONS OF MY PRACTICE

What guides our work

01

Person-Centred

I aim to offer empathy, genuine connection, and respect for your autonomy, recognizing that you have an important voice in your care.

02

Trauma-Informed

I prioritize emotional safety, choice, collaboration, and respect for your boundaries throughout the counselling process.

03

Collaborative

We work together to identify goals, explore experiences, and find approaches that make sense for your individual needs.

04

Culturally Humble

Your identities, culture, relationships, and lived experiences deserve to be acknowledged with openness and respect.

05

Strengths-Based

We make room for challenges while also recognizing your resilience, resources, and capacity for growth.

06

Flexible & Integrative

Counselling is adapted to your needs, preferences, and evolving goals rather than following a rigid formula.

THERAPEUTIC APPROACHES

Drawing from different perspectives.

Depending on your needs, I may integrate ideas and strategies from cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT), dialectical behaviour therapy (DBT), solution-focused brief therapy, narrative therapy, psychodynamic approaches, and motivational interviewing.

These approaches offer different ways to understand emotions, relationships, patterns, and opportunities for change.

We can discuss what feels helpful, and your feedback will always be welcome.

✧

What can you expect?

Our first sessions are an opportunity to get to know each other and understand what has brought you to counselling.

You don't need to prepare the perfect explanation or know exactly what your goals are. We can take time to explore those questions together.

I welcome questions, feedback, and conversations about what is or isn't working for you. Your comfort and involvement matter throughout the process.
