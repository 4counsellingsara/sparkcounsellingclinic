WORKING TOGETHER

My Approach to Counselling

A compassionate, collaborative space where your experiences are honoured, your voice matters, and meaningful change happens at your own pace.

MY PHILOSOPHY

You are the expert on your own experience.

I believe counselling works best when we approach it as a partnership.

You bring your experiences, values, and understanding of yourself. I bring curiosity, compassion, clinical knowledge, and a commitment to creating a supportive space.

Rather than assuming I know what's best for you, I want to understand what matters to you and explore possible paths forward together.

THERAPEUTIC APPROACHES

Drawing from different perspectives.

Depending on your needs, I may integrate ideas and strategies from cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT), dialectical behaviour therapy (DBT), solution-focused brief therapy, narrative therapy, psychodynamic approaches, and motivational interviewing.

These approaches offer different ways to understand emotions, relationships, patterns, and opportunities for change. Your feedback is always welcome.

BEGINNING COUNSELLING

What can you expect?

Starting counselling can feel unfamiliar, especially if it's your first time reaching out for support. You don't need to know exactly what to say or have everything figured out before we meet.

Our first sessions are an opportunity to get to know each other, explore what brought you to counselling, and begin understanding what you'd like to work toward.

Before your first counselling appointment, you'll receive intake and informed consent forms to review and complete.

These forms help me better understand your needs and provide important information about the counselling process, confidentiality, fees, policies, and your rights.

Throughout our work together, I welcome questions and feedback about what is or isn't working for you. Counselling is a collaborative process, and your voice matters every step of the way.

If you're unsure whether counselling is right for you, you're welcome to begin with a complimentary 15-minute phone consultation to ask questions and explore whether working together feels like a good fit.
