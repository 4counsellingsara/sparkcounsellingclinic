A SPACE FOR YOU

Come as you are.

At Spark Counselling Clinic in Kelowna, British Columbia, I believe everyone deserves a space where they feel heard, understood, and accepted.

Counselling isn't about having all the answers or becoming someone different. It's about reconnecting with yourself, discovering your strengths, and finding a way forward that feels meaningful to you.

Whether you're experiencing anxiety, depression, relationship challenges, difficult life experiences, or significant life transitions, counselling offers an opportunity to explore your experiences with compassion and curiosity.

Wherever you are in your journey, you're welcome here.

PERSONALIZED SUPPORT

Support that meets you where you are.

Every person brings their own experiences, perspectives, and needs into counselling.

My approach is collaborative, compassionate, and tailored to what matters most to you. Together, we'll explore your experiences and identify meaningful ways forward.

Through a person-centred and trauma-informed approach, I integrate counselling strategies that support emotional well-being, self-understanding, resilience, and personal growth.

THE PERSON BEHIND SPARK

Hi, I'm Sara.

I'm Sara Park, the counsellor behind Spark Counselling Clinic, based in Kelowna, British Columbia.

I believe meaningful change begins with feeling genuinely seen, heard, and understood. My goal is to create a welcoming, nonjudgmental space where you can explore your experiences at your own pace.

My practice is grounded in a person-centred, trauma-informed approach. I value collaboration, curiosity, cultural humility, and recognizing the strengths you already carry within you.

I draw from evidence-informed approaches, including Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT), Solution-Focused Brief Therapy (SFBT), narrative therapy, and psychodynamic perspectives, depending on your individual needs and goals.

Together, we can explore what matters to you and work toward meaningful, lasting change.
